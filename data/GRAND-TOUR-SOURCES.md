# Grand Tour encore: stops 10–19

These ten stops extend the original nine-stop route in both the browser and terminal. The tour uses destinations already present in the compiled atlas. Its flights and close approaches are visual navigation, not physical spacecraft trajectories. The solar-system positions are fixed scene positions; the surfaces below are illustrations except for Mars's compiled NASA source map. Nebula and quasar geometry is illustrative at observed sky anchors.

| Stop | Sight | Observational basis | Atlas view |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | Mars | [NASA Mars facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/) describe Olympus Mons and Valles Marineris. | NASA source surface map compiled for the atlas. |
| 11 | Jupiter | [NASA Jupiter facts](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-facts/) describe its cloud bands and Great Red Spot. | Authored band and storm illustration. |
| 12 | Io | [NASA Io facts](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-moons/io/facts/) identify Io as the most volcanically active world in the Solar System. | Authored volcanic surface illustration. |
| 13 | Europa | [NASA's Galileo mission](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/galileo/galileo-science/) found evidence of a salty ocean beneath cracked ice. | Authored ice and fracture illustration. |
| 14 | Titan | [NASA Titan facts](https://science.nasa.gov/saturn/moons/titan/facts/) describe its dense atmosphere and surface lakes and seas of hydrocarbons. | Authored haze and surface colors; no mapped shorelines. |
| 15 | Enceladus | [NASA's Cassini findings](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/cassini/science/enceladus/) describe south polar water ice and vapor plumes and a subsurface ocean. | Authored ice surface; the plumes are described, not depicted. |
| 16 | Neptune | [NASA Neptune facts](https://science.nasa.gov/neptune/neptune-facts/) describe its methane-influenced color and exceptionally strong winds. | Authored blue atmospheric bands. |
| 17 | Pluto | [NASA's New Horizons imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/frozen-plains-in-the-heart-of-plutos-heart/) reveals bright plains within Tombaugh Regio. | Illustrated dwarf-planet surface; it does not reproduce the observed heart. |
| 18 | Crab Nebula | [NASA Webb's overview](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/investigating-the-origins-of-the-crab-nebula-with-nasas-webb/) describes the remnant of the supernova observed in 1054. | Authored three-dimensional emission field at the observed anchor. |
| 19 | 3C 273 | [NASA Hubble's overview](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/hubble/quasar-3c-273-2/) describes the quasar, its jet and its approximate distance. | Conceptual disk and jet with compressed visualization scales. |

The astronomical anchors and rendering limits for Crab Nebula and 3C 273 are recorded in [PHENOMENA-SOURCES.md](PHENOMENA-SOURCES.md). Solar surface assets and their source classes are recorded in `artifacts/surface-metadata.json`.
